## 💡 WHY YOU CAN'T MAKE MONEY ON SOCIAL MEDIA DIRECTLY

### The Problem:
1. **Facebook/Instagram**: You don't own the audience
   - Algorithms control reach
   - Can't monetize directly (unless huge following: 10,000+ followers)
   - FB Creator Studio requires: 10K followers + 600K minutes viewed

2. **YouTube**: Requires thresholds
   - 1,000 subscribers
   - 4,000 watch hours in past 12 months
   - Even then, CPM is low in India (₹20-100 per 1000 views)

3. **LinkedIn/Pinterest**: No direct monetization
   - Can only drive traffic elsewhere

### The Solution: YOUR WEBSITE
**Your website is YOUR ASSET**:
- ✅ You control 100% of revenue
- ✅ Multiple monetization methods
- ✅ No algorithm restrictions
- ✅ Build email list (owned audience)
- ✅ No follower thresholds for ad revenue
- ✅ Higher revenue per visitor

**Strategy**: Use social media as TRAFFIC SOURCE → Drive to website → Monetize on website
