### 11. Common Issues & Solutions

**Issue**: Payment failures common
**Solution**: 
- Multiple payment method options
- Retry mechanism after 30 seconds
- Clear error messages in Hindi/English
- SMS notifications for payment status

**Issue**: Customers worried about legitimacy
**Solution**:
- Display GST registration prominently
- Show company registration details
- Testimonials from verified customers
- Trust badges and certifications

**Issue**: Refund disputes
**Solution**:
- Automated refund process
- Clear refund policy (not in small print)
- Quick processing (5-7 days)
- Direct communication channel
